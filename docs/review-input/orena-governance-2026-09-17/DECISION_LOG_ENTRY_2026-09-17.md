# DECISION_LOG entry — 2026-09-17

## Orena product/design recovery direction

**Decision:** Reaffirm Orena as a content-rich language-learning product with a simple beginner-safe front door and progressively deeper discovery.

### Durable principles

- Clarity first. Curiosity next. Depth over time.
- Simple front door. Deep world behind it.
- "World" is an internal design principle, not learner-facing lore or fantasy.
- Guided learning and open exploration coexist.
- Clear navigation labels remain valid; personality must not reduce comprehension.
- Less words, more life.
- Artwork becomes a first-class design system governed by a shared Art Bible.
- Content imagery, covers, thumbnails, icons, color and motion should carry more of the emotional/visual load than slogans.
- Avoid repeated hero-header/slogan templates.
- Library stores the world; Discover reveals it; learning tools help learners interact with it.
- Skills remain capabilities/shortcuts but should not be the only organizational model for content.
- New learners must always have an obvious next action.
- Focused learning modes reduce shell noise and prioritize content/task.
- English and Chinese remain first-class.

### Explicit non-goals

- no fantasy/lore redesign;
- no blanket removal of Reading/Listening/etc.;
- no redesign-by-decoration;
- no random vivid palette;
- no generic SaaS dashboard;
- no philosophy explanation to learners;
- no production placeholder artwork.

### Reason

The current product contains many strong individual pieces but risks drifting toward poetic copy over conventional SaaS architecture, while artwork is inconsistent and copy/hero patterns are overused. This decision restores the intended direction without sacrificing beginner clarity.
