# Billing / Monetization lane — risk register

This register lists meaningful residual risks only. The leader owns it, and changes it only when a risk opens,
changes or closes.

| ID | Severity | Risk | Owner | Status | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-MON-001 | P0 | A verified payment changes no plan. Billing writes `commerce_subscriptions`, but the plan and quota read the legacy `subscriptions` table. | lane | closed: PR-MON-1 merged (#153, `aa016dc1`) | One effective-entitlement resolver (D-MON-006, D-MON-010..014), with precedence tests and a PostgreSQL proof. |
| R-MON-002 | P1 | Money is taken and nothing is granted, with no durable trace. A verified payOS mismatch, unknown order or deleted account answers 200 and leaves only a log line. | lane | mitigated: PR-MON-3 merged (#178, `1a9e010d`); effective once 0027 + 0031 are promoted and applied (human gate) | D-MON-004, D-MON-018: every verified anomaly becomes a durable reconciliation item before it is acknowledged (503 when it cannot be); the reconciler repairs prepaid access gaps, opens a critical item that closes the gateway's checkouts when a repair fails, and asks payOS about every unsettled order. Billing stays off until promotion and activation. |
| R-MON-003 | P1 | A trial or Free allowance is reset by deleting and re-registering. A new account incarnation would start with empty buckets. | lane | unresolved, PR-MON-6 | Bind promotional allowances to an identity that survives re-registration (the `users` row / sign-in subject). The design is reviewed with PR-MON-6. |
| R-MON-004 | P1 | The price shown and the price charged drift. The catalogue and `BILLING_PRICES_FILE` are independent. | lane | closed: PR-MON-2 merged (#162, `ea68f8a8`) | D-MON-005, D-MON-015: every approved price is compared with the catalogue in exact minor units; one mismatch blocks offers and checkout on its whole gateway, and a checkout starts only at the price the learner confirmed. The merchant-of-record side stays open (R-MON-011). |
| R-MON-005 | P2 | An expensive AI path bypasses admission. Only the four bucket meters are wired; other AI paths are bound by spend caps, not plan quota. | lane | unresolved, PR-MON-6/7 | Inventory every provider call site. Prove that no paid dispatch can happen without admission (brief §19, cost protection). |
| R-MON-006 | P2 | A Polar renewal fails or its webhook is late, and a paying learner loses access at once. | lane | partly mitigated (D-MON-011) | A failed renewal gets a 48-hour `past_due` grace, anchored on the end of the paid period, never on arrival time. Still open: a successful renewal whose webhook arrives after `paid_through` reads Free until it lands, because grace covers `past_due` only. This is accepted for v1; Polar retries webhook delivery, and PR-MON-8 measures the delay. |
| R-MON-008 | P3 | The grace number is environment-configured, not Admin-editable, so changing it needs a restart. | lane + UI lane | accepted for Billing v1 | Promote it to `writing_coach/policies.py` once the Admin policies page has its EN/VI/ZH copy. A typo falls back to the approved 48 hours and is logged. |
| R-MON-009 | P2 | Polar's past-due payload fields (`past_due_at`, `current_period_start`) are taken from Polar's public docs, not a captured sandbox payload. | lane | unresolved, PR-MON-8 (confirmed by review P2) | The adapter falls back to the last stored paid end when both are absent. Verify against a real Polar sandbox `subscription.past_due` event in PR-MON-8. |
| R-MON-010 | P2 | An unreadable table listing at startup installs no billing source. That looks the same as a billing-less deployment, so a paid account reads the legacy row for the life of the process. | lane | closed: PR-MON-2 merged (#162, `ea68f8a8`) | D-MON-016: where commerce is expected (PostgreSQL with the account backbone asked for), the billing source is installed whatever the startup listing said. Listed absent reads no billing row; an unreadable listing fails every plan read closed and retries, and readiness names it (`entitlement_source_unverified`, `commerce_schema_unreadable`). |
| R-MON-007 | P2 | Cross-lane merge conflicts in `product/service.py`, `membership.py` and `app.py`, which the UI and other lanes also touch. | lane | unresolved | Keep changes minimal and response shapes unchanged. Rebase on `main` before review and note overlaps in `BILLING_STATUS.md`. |
| R-MON-011 | P2 | Polar charges the price configured on Polar's side for `external_price_id`. PR-MON-2 compares the prices file's amount with the catalogue, not with what Polar will charge. | lane | unresolved, PR-MON-8 / PR-MON-3 | Verify each Polar price id's amount and currency against the file in the Polar sandbox (PR-MON-8), and record a verified subscription whose amount differs as a payment anomaly (PR-MON-3). |
| R-MON-012 | P2 | Only part of the commerce schema is present (some 0006 tables missing). `table_state` reports that as `absent`, the same as commerce never deployed, so it reads no billing row instead of failing closed. | lane | accepted for v1 (PR-MON-2 review P2) | Migrations are atomic, so a partial schema is a broken deployment. Later, report `partial` separately and fail closed on it. |
| R-MON-013 | P2 | The payOS lookup by `orderCode`/`paymentLinkId`, `expiredAt`, `amountPaid` and the statuses PAID/PENDING/PROCESSING/CANCELLED are documented by payOS (per the PR-MON-3 review; re-read at implementation). The exact transaction-reference shape and any other status (`EXPIRED` included) are not. | lane | narrowed, PR-MON-8 | The PR-MON-3 idempotency model does not depend on them (one payment per payOS order, pull id `payos:order:<code>`, confirming lookup for overpayment); unknown statuses are non-terminal. Verify in the payOS sandbox before production activation. |
