## What changed in the design (line diff against the previous pin: 235 diff lines)

- **Plan & usage** (`data-screen-label="Plan & usage"`, the `billing` route): one new card is inserted before the payment
  method card: the Credits card (spark mark tile, the balance, "left", a one-line explanation, "Add Credits" and
  "View history"). The rest of the frame is unchanged.
- **Credits** (new frame, `data-screen-label="Credits"`, route `credits`, added to the focus list in `applyBody`): header
  with back and a "Plan & usage" button; a balance hero; a strip naming the plan beside "Credits are separate"; an
  optional pending banner with "Show QR"; the pack picker (radio cards with total, a benefit line, optional tag and price),
  a summary and "Continue" button, a "VietQR ... via payOS" line; History (All / Added / Used filter, day groups, rows);
  "How Credits work" (five rows) and a prototype note ("Payments are simulated").
- **Credits Payment** (new frame, `data-screen-label="Credits Payment"`, the `crSheet` sheet): steps `confirm`, `qr`
  (QR, amount, transfer note with Copy, "Waiting for your transfer" with a 15:00 timer and a prototype-only "Let this QR
  expire"), `received` ("Payment received ... Adding"), `done` ("Credits added", new balance, a receipt line) and
  `expired`. `closeAll` and `anySheet` cover it.
- **Profile**: the action list gains a "Credits" row (balance, "top up, history") after "Plan & usage".
- **State script**: new `CR_PACKS`, `CR_SEED`, `CR_IC`, `CR_QR` (sample data), `crBalance`, `crCount`, `crRaw`,
  `crStopTimers`, `crPay`, `crDetect` (the prototype's simulated payment: it "detects" the transfer after 8 seconds),
  `creditVals` and the Vietnamese strings for them.
- **Tokens**: four new colour tokens in both themes (`--spark`, `--spark-ink`, `--spark-soft`, `--spark-line`), the
  gold of the Credits spark (dark `#F7BE5E`/`#FFD58A`/`#2A2215`/`#4B3B1C`, light `#EFAE45`/`#8A5300`/`#FFF5E2`/`#F0D7A6`).
  The keyframes `crGlint`, `crPulse`, `crScan`, `crRing`, `crFly`, `crPop` (stopped under `prefers-reduced-motion`) and
  the `#cr-spark` symbol with its gradient are new.
- **Editor artefact**: the export carries a 12 KB `<style data-omelette-injected>` and `<script data-omelette-injected>` block
  (lines 4-6) that the previous pin did not have. It is the design host's own tooling (theme query parameter, console
  relay, a `window.claude` shim); it is pinned as exported, byte for byte, and nothing in the app reads it.

Not built from the design (see `UI_BACKEND_GAPS.md` "Credits, Credits Payment"): the bonus and "Most chosen" tag, the
receipt line, the simulated-payment affordances, the transfer note. Sample content (pack sizes and prices, history) is
never shipped (D-068).
