## What the new frames are

**Vocabulary (32).** The library and its card, then the review loop as a whole
life: the card itself, four more review modes the app does not have (typing,
listen-and-choose, dictation, cloze, speaking, with a mic-blocked frame),
**Review summary** desktop and mobile, search, save-to-deck, add word, create
deck, review settings, and four states - empty, nothing due, offline, load
error.

**Speaking (18).** Free-talk result, lesson summary, compare with the model,
shadowing, settings, and the failures a microphone actually has: blocked, not
heard, offline grading, empty.

**Thư viện của tôi (26).** One place for everything a learner kept, across
types: review, item detail for a word / a piece of writing / a speaking take,
lists per type, search, collections (detail, list, create, add-to sheet),
multi-select and delete, and the states - empty, all done, loading, offline.

**Admin Control Center (21).** The console's six states, four detail screens,
drawers and confirms, credentials, import history, operations, and how admin is
entered from a profile - including "no access".
