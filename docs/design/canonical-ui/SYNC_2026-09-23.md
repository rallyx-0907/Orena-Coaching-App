## Rules the design states that the implementation must keep

- **A configured provider with no traffic is `NO DATA`, not healthy.** The
  design writes this out on the AI metrics panel.
- **Simulated figures are labelled as such** on the canvas itself ("Dữ liệu mô
  phỏng để dựng luồng — không phải số liệu thật"), which is the same rule D-066
  already sets for the product: no demo numbers reach a real screen.
- **Future controls are shown and disabled**, marked `TƯƠNG LAI` (future):
  source polling, coverage, and the automation block on a source. They are part
  of the design, not of today's runtime.
- **Actions that the backend cannot do are named and disabled** rather than
  omitted — "Đổi vai trò · chưa khả dụng" (change role · not available yet).
- **Evidence is collapsed, not hidden:** processing evidence and technical
  detail are `thu gọn` (collapsed) by default on review and job detail.
- **Nothing waits in a modal.** Add content queues the work and closes.
