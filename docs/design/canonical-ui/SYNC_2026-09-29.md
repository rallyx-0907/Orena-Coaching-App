## How the copy was made

`Orena Admin.dc.html` is 228 005 bytes, under DesignSync's 256 KiB cap. It was read with
`DesignSync get_file` (`truncated: false`), its content written as UTF-8 bytes, and those bytes
are **byte-identical** to the human's export of 2026-09-27 (`Downloads/orena-design/`), SHA-256
`c41aa8a04b5fe26e57132d585124fb084fd5af09a7f6b543f58d6fd739a8b056`. The pinned file is those
bytes.

The brief was read with `get_file` too (`truncated: false`, 42 404 bytes in the export); its 35
section headings, first and last lines and the checked passages match the export, which is what
is pinned. That comparison was by sample, not by hash.
